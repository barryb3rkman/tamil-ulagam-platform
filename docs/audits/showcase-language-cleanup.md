# Showcase Language Cleanup

## Purpose

This review presents Tamil Ulagam as one coherent public vision while preserving the boundaries required for an informational website. The source presentation remains the authority for the federation’s vision, objectives, initiative structure and terminology. The cleanup does not establish operational availability, named relationships, public launch dates or numerical targets.

## Routes reviewed

The showcase review covers:

- `/`
- `/about`
- `/initiatives`
- all eight initiative detail routes
- `/tamil-id`
- `/chapters`
- `/partners`
- `/events`
- `/news`
- `/contact`

The shared header, footer, calls to action, image captions and route metadata were included in the review. `/roadmap`, `/privacy` and `/terms` were reviewed as intentional exceptions.

## Language treatment

Visible status-report, backlog and approval-workflow wording was removed from showcase routes. This includes repeated labels and sentences built around planned, proposed, in-development, unavailable, future-phase, concept-preview and readiness terminology. Affected sentences were rewritten as complete, direct descriptions rather than shortened through blind word removal.

Status badges and their now-empty layout wrappers were removed from showcase heroes, initiative cards, chapter regions and feature panels. Hero rhythm, card spacing and call-to-action placement continue to use the existing design system.

Metadata titles and descriptions now describe each showcase route directly without draft, planned, proposed, future, in-development or concept labels.

## Route-specific outcomes

- The homepage presents the federation, objectives, Tamil ID, initiatives, chapters, roadmap, mobile experience, collaboration and stories as a unified ecosystem.
- About retains the vision, mission, global challenge, six objectives, digital ecosystem and cultural direction without rollout commentary.
- Initiatives presents all eight initiative identities and their capability sets without service-status badges or transactional claims.
- Tamil ID presents a QR-enabled membership credential, membership tiers and connected access without implying issuance, universal eligibility, lifetime validity or acceptance by third parties.
- Chapters presents chapter regions, local community value and federation connection without active chapter counts or application claims.
- Partners presents collaboration categories and forms of contribution with no named or implied confirmed partners.
- Events presents discovery, annual celebrations and community participation without dates, listings, registration, ticketing or recordings claims.
- News presents announcements, community news, diaspora updates, bilingual communication and editorial responsibility without fabricated articles, dates or authors.
- Contact presents enquiry context, categories and useful preparation guidance without inventing a form, email address, telephone number or physical address.

## Safety and truthfulness retained

The following boundaries remain visible:

- Tamil ID is not a government identity document and is not proof of citizenship, nationality or travel authority.
- The healthcare initiative is not an emergency or crisis-support service and does not provide medical care or telemedicine.
- Contact guidance warns against sharing identity documents or sensitive personal information through public routes and directs emergencies to appropriate local services.
- Public discussion or inclusion does not imply endorsement, contract or confirmed partnership.
- Chapter recognition does not imply control over existing Tamil organisations, and listed regions are not represented as operating chapters.

The site does not claim app-store availability, active chapter totals, confirmed partners, live registrations, payments, submissions or active service delivery.

## Intentional exceptions

The Roadmap retains Foundation, Connected Community and Global Services, together with phase labels, development sequencing and readiness language. Its purpose is to communicate progression rather than showcase-route availability.

Privacy and Terms retain their draft-for-legal-review labels, not-yet-approved notices, unresolved legal facts and pending organisation or contact information. These warnings must remain until qualified legal and governance review supplies the missing decisions.

## Visual and responsive review

Every showcase route and all three exception routes are reviewed at desktop and mobile sizes. The review checks hero spacing after badge removal, empty wrappers, card rhythm, call-to-action placement, footer wording, mobile readability, horizontal overflow and the continued visual distinction of legal and Roadmap content.

## Remaining factual and legal decisions

No showcase copy resolves the outstanding legal-entity, governing-law, contact-detail, privacy-controller or policy-effective-date decisions recorded by the legal drafts. Operational rules for membership issuance, QR verification, chapters, partners, events, publishing and initiative delivery also require responsible governance before any corresponding workflow is introduced.

## Final tone assessment

The public showcase now speaks in a direct institutional voice: it explains what Tamil Ulagam is, the ecosystem it brings together and the value it is designed to create. Necessary boundaries are concise and local to the relevant subject, rather than repeated as site-wide development notices.
