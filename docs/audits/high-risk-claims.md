## Launch position

The current website generally reduces source risk by omitting numbers and names and by using planned-status language. Launch remains conditional on approval of the organisation’s official name/status, brand and mission, legal operator, public contact ownership, and the legal-page draft status. No source assertion about active services, apps, members, chapters, partners, dates or measured impact should be restored without evidence.
