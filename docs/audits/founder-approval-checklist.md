# Founder approval checklist

Use this checklist to record explicit decisions. A checked item means a decision has been documented; it does not replace specialist, governance, legal or factual review.

## Brand and mission

- [ ] Confirm the official name “Tamil Ulagam Global Federation”.
- [ ] Confirm whether “Global Federation” is a brand descriptor, organisational form or legal-status statement.
- [ ] Approve the public vision wording.
- [ ] Approve the public mission wording.
- [ ] Approve the six objectives: Connect, Preserve, Empower, Support, Foster and Celebrate.
- [ ] Approve the website’s editorial taglines and decide whether the PPT tagline “Connecting the Global Tamil Community” remains official.
- [ ] Approve the Tamil quotation used on the About page, its attribution and English translation after language/editorial review.

## Organisational status

- [ ] Identify the legal entity and public website operator.
- [ ] Confirm whether federation status is legally established and how it may be described.
- [ ] Confirm represented countries, if any; do not infer representation from diaspora presence.
- [ ] Document which operations are active and which remain proposals.
- [ ] Confirm who is authorised to speak for Tamil communities globally and constrain public wording accordingly.

## Platform scope

- [ ] Approve the permanent public website scope.
- [ ] Approve or defer the mobile application.
- [ ] Approve or defer membership and Tamil ID.
- [ ] Approve or defer chapters and organisation directories.
- [ ] Approve or defer event, news, partner-portal and administrative capabilities.
- [ ] Approve each of the eight initiative domains and identify accountable owners.
- [ ] Confirm that no CTA may imply an operational application, registration, payment or submission path before launch approval.

## Tamil ID

- [ ] Decide whether Community, Professional and Patron tiers exist.
- [ ] Approve eligibility rules; the PPT’s universal eligibility is not currently adopted.
- [ ] Decide whether membership is free or paid.
- [ ] Decide credential duration, renewal and expiry; lifetime validity is not currently adopted.
- [ ] Approve identity and membership verification requirements.
- [ ] Approve QR behaviour, information disclosure and anti-enumeration/security controls.
- [ ] Confirm partner acceptance only through written agreements.
- [ ] Approve public/private member-data boundaries and visibility choices.
- [ ] Approve the explicit statement that Tamil ID is not a government passport, nationality, immigration or statutory identity document.
- [ ] Obtain privacy, security and legal approval before data collection or issuance.

## Chapters

- [ ] Verify whether any chapter is active or formally recognised.
- [ ] Approve country/city chapter structures and territorial boundaries.
- [ ] Approve leadership eligibility, appointment/election and succession rules.
- [ ] Define recognition authority and public representation rights.
- [ ] Approve governance, conflicts, finance, audit and complaint requirements.
- [ ] Approve suspension, renewal and closure processes with fair review.

## Partnerships

- [ ] Approve the categories of institution Tamil Ulagam may engage.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation before naming any government, ministry or UNESCO relationship.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation before naming any university.
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation before naming any Tamil association.
- [ ] Approve logo/name permissions and public wording separately from exploratory discussions.
- [ ] Designate partnership approval authority.
- [ ] Approve proportionate due diligence, conflicts, data access and termination rules.
- [ ] Approve the process for public partnership announcements.

## Events

- [ ] Decide whether and when an event calendar will launch.
- [ ] Approve or reject each named annual event in the PPT.
- [ ] Approve whether a Global Tamil Summit or awards night will exist.
- [ ] Define organiser eligibility and verification.
- [ ] Approve registration, ticketing and payment/refund rules before activation.
- [ ] Approve photography, recording, streaming and archive consent rules.
- [ ] Approve safeguarding, accessibility, moderation, cancellation and complaints procedures.

## News and editorial

- [ ] Appoint accountable editorial ownership.
- [ ] Define which statements are official announcements.
- [ ] Approve article/community contribution intake before accepting submissions.
- [ ] Approve corrections, updates, withdrawals, retractions and archive rules.
- [ ] Approve authorship, attribution and conflicts disclosures.
- [ ] Approve Tamil/English translation ownership and equivalence checks.
- [ ] Approve consent and safeguarding for identifiable community stories.

## Initiatives

- [ ] Approve healthcare scope and obtain clinical/legal governance before any service.
- [ ] Approve education scope, child safeguarding and content ownership.
- [ ] Approve business scope and exclude unlicensed transaction/investment activity.
- [ ] Approve jobs scope, employer verification and recommendation fairness.
- [ ] Approve research scope, archive rights, grants and institutional relationships.
- [ ] Approve tourism scope, supplier safety and commercial responsibilities.
- [ ] Approve culture, awards, rights, consent and youth safeguards.
- [ ] Approve global-events scope and operating governance.
- [ ] Select first pilot priorities only after owners, evidence, resources and exit criteria exist.

## Roadmap

- [ ] Decide whether any dates can be made public.
- [ ] Approve phase order and dependencies.
- [ ] Decide mobile and membership timing.
- [ ] Decide Tamil ID and chapter timing.
- [ ] Approve any chapter/member/initiative targets before publication.
- [ ] Reconcile the PPT’s conflicting one-crore target dates (2027 and 2029).
- [ ] Approve impact targets only with baselines, methods, owners and evidence.

## Contact

- [ ] Verify control of the source website domain.
- [ ] Verify the source email owner and decide whether a role-based address is required.
- [ ] Verify the source phone owner, monitoring and permitted public use.
- [ ] Name authorised representatives and escalation owners.
- [ ] Confirm whether any source contact detail remains current.
- [ ] Approve privacy notice, retention, access and response expectations before accepting enquiries.

## Legal and privacy

- [ ] Confirm legal entity, operator and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Appoint privacy ownership and a contact/complaint route.
- [ ] Map actual data processing, purposes and legal bases.
- [ ] Identify providers, processing locations and transfer safeguards.
- [ ] Approve retention/deletion schedules.
- [ ] Decide children’s access, consent and safeguarding requirements.
- [ ] Approve complaint, appeal and enforcement routes.
- [ ] Approve service-specific terms before accounts, identity, events, content, payments or partnerships launch.
- [ ] Approve governing law, dispute handling and mandatory consumer rights.
- [ ] Set effective/revision dates only after qualified legal review.

## Statistics and impact

- [ ] Evidence or remove the 80M population claim.
- [ ] Evidence or remove the 100+ countries claim.
- [ ] Evidence or qualify the 2,000+ years claim.
- [ ] Evidence or remove the diaspora ranking.
- [ ] Evidence or remove the ₹50,000 Cr contribution claim.
- [ ] Verify chapter counts and locations before publication.
- [ ] Approve membership targets only with a reconciled date and forecast.
- [ ] Approve jobs, student, healthcare and trade targets only with definitions, baselines and measurement owners.

## Decision record fields

For every approval, record the decision, approver, date, evidence, scope, conditions, review date and superseded wording. Unchecked items remain unapproved.
