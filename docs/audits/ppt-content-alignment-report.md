# PPT content alignment report

## Purpose and source boundary

This report records the public-content alignment completed against `source-materials/Tamil-Ulagam-Source.pptx`. The presentation was the sole primary content source. The existing source audit and claim registers were used as traceability aids; they do not independently verify any statement in the presentation.

The alignment preserves the approved visual system, navigation, route structure, accessibility foundations, image registry, responsive behaviour and draft legal treatment. It is an editorial realignment rather than a slide-by-slide transcription.

## Summary of changes

- Restored the source presentation's central framing, “Connecting the Global Tamil Community,” to the homepage.
- Restored the six objectives: Connect, Preserve, Empower, Support, Foster and Celebrate.
- Aligned the eight initiative names and capability sets with the source presentation.
- Reframed Tamil ID around proposed digital membership, QR-enabled identity, membership tiers and future access while retaining clear non-government and non-issuance boundaries.
- Replaced the expanded six-stage roadmap with the source presentation's three-phase structure, without fixed dates or the unresolved one-crore target.
- Added the proposed chapter regions and six proposed annual-event concepts from the presentation with explicit non-operational status.
- Aligned partnership categories, news themes and contact pathways to the presentation while omitting unverified names and contact details.
- Removed detailed public-page readiness checklists, stage models, administrative workflows and governance explanations that exceeded the presentation's public vision.

## Invented detail removed or simplified

The public routes no longer render the following expanded models where they were not required by the source presentation:

- About-page institutional governance detail.
- Initiatives overview platform-layer and readiness-principle sections.
- Initiative-specific multi-stage development paths and extensive readiness checklists.
- Tamil ID application journey, detailed verification workflow, governance model and staged rollout.
- Chapter formation journey, recognition stages, responsibility matrix, governance controls and readiness model.
- Roadmap dependency architecture, platform layers, quality gates and change-control sections.
- Partnership lifecycle, due-diligence process, governance/access model, relationship status model and readiness checklist.
- Event organiser pathway, publication lifecycle, registration model, status model and readiness framework.
- Newsroom workflow, source-verification process, authorship model, distribution model and readiness framework.
- Contact-routing workflow, response model and administrative submission guidance.
- Repetitive FAQ entries that restated the same unavailable-service or internal-process boundaries.

Concise editorial explanation remains where it helps visitors understand the source vision without creating a new service promise.

## Legal, privacy and safety content retained

- Tamil ID remains explicitly separate from government identity, citizenship, nationality and legal status.
- Tamil ID remains a concept with no current issuance or public QR verification service.
- The Healthcare page retains a concise statement that Tamil Ulagam does not provide medical care or telemedicine and directs emergencies to local emergency services.
- The Contact page retains the sensitive-document warning, no-emergency-service statement and absence of a live form.
- Events retains the absence of a live calendar.
- News retains the absence of an approved public article collection and concise future consent/correction principles.
- Partners and Chapters retain explicit boundaries against implying confirmed relationships or active chapters.
- Privacy and Terms remain draft foundations requiring qualified legal review.

## High-risk claims omitted or softened

The alignment does not publish the presentation's population, country, economic-contribution, chapter, membership, jobs, education, healthcare or trade figures as current facts. It also omits:

- the conflicting one-crore membership dates;
- fixed public delivery dates;
- named government, UNESCO, university, association or corporate relationships;
- claims of active app-store availability;
- lifetime or universally free Tamil ID membership;
- universal eligibility or partner acceptance;
- active healthcare, telemedicine, jobs, marketplace, grants, awards or event services;
- the source email address and telephone number pending ownership confirmation.

Where a source concept is retained, it is labelled Planned, Proposed, In development, Future phase or Concept as appropriate.

## Remaining deviations from the presentation

The website deliberately retains editorial explanations, intended-audience summaries, accessibility language, legal cautions and safety boundaries that are not slide text. These additions clarify meaning and reduce risk without changing the source vision.

The website also deliberately excludes or softens presentation claims that remain unverified, internally inconsistent or operationally unapproved. This means the site is faithful to the presentation's direction but not to every numerical, institutional or availability assertion.

## Visual review

Twenty full-page review captures were produced under `artifacts/ppt-content-alignment-review/` for Home, About, Initiatives, Tamil ID, Chapters, Roadmap, Partners, Events, News and Contact at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844.

The final review found:

- no horizontal overflow, clipped headings, broken media or empty composition gaps;
- clear planned/proposed status treatment at both viewports;
- reduced page density after removing non-source workflow sections and repetitive FAQ entries;
- consistent section rhythm, readable mobile stacking and retained premium visual hierarchy;
- no oversized warning panel beyond the concise safety boundaries required on Healthcare and Contact;
- no visual treatment that presents proposed services, chapters, partners, events or publishing as active.

The capture directory is excluded from version control and is not part of the public asset tree.

## Launch recommendation

The aligned content is suitable for continued public-site review because it now reflects the source presentation more directly while keeping planned capabilities honest. Public launch should remain conditional on founder approval of the organisation's official identity and core wording, confirmation of the responsible operator and public contact channel, and qualified legal review. Unverified statistics, partners, chapters, dates, services and impact targets should remain unpublished until supported by documented evidence and approval.
