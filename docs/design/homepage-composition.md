# Homepage composition

The public homepage is intentionally a long-form editorial introduction to Tamil Ulagam, not a dashboard or service marketplace. It moves from the federation’s promise to its three principles, then introduces the planned identity, initiative, chapter, roadmap, mobile and partnership directions before closing with a clear invitation to participate.

The twelve sections use the approved registry images through `ImageWithFallback`. Only the responsive hero pair is above the fold and eager; every other image preserves its verified dimensions and remains lazy loaded. Concept imagery is labelled as a preview, and planned initiatives use explicit status badges.

The layout uses deep navy feature bands, warm ivory reading surfaces, restrained maroon and gold markers, large editorial type and asymmetric grids. CSS transitions are limited to focus and hover details, and all motion remains optional under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

On narrow screens, every grid becomes a deliberate vertical sequence. The hero switches to its approved mobile composition through the shared picture implementation, and no critical content relies on text over an image.
