# Contact page composition

## Purpose and current state

The Contact page provides a calm public guide for beginning future conversations with Tamil Ulagam. It distinguishes general, partnership, chapter, initiative, event, editorial, membership, privacy, institutional and website-feedback enquiries without presenting a live form or publishing unapproved contact details.

Typed content in `apps/web/src/content/contact.ts` defines the hero, enquiry guidance, categories, useful context, sensitive-information boundaries, routing, privacy, response expectations, emergency limitation, institutional authority, proposed workflow, FAQs and final public-page links.

## Boundaries and responsibility

The page states that contacting Tamil Ulagam does not create an application, membership, chapter or partnership. It asks visitors not to send identity documents, financial information, medical records, credentials or other unnecessary sensitive material. Future routing is framed around data minimisation, purpose limitation, restricted access, responsible ownership and controlled handover.

The emergency section makes clear that Tamil Ulagam is not an emergency or crisis-response service. Institutional guidance separates early discussion from approval, representation rights, logo use and access to private member information.

## Responsive, accessibility and metadata strategy

The text-led hero uses restrained geometry and no image request. Editorial directories and ordered stages become vertical reading sequences on small screens, with semantic headings, lists, static FAQ markup, visible status text and established focus treatment. Metadata uses the canonical Contact route without contact-point, telephone, address or office-hour data. The static page introduces no form, map, client component, dependency or new animation behavior.
