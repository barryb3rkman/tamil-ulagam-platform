# Chapters Page Composition

## Purpose and positioning

The Chapters page explains how a future Tamil Ulagam chapter network may connect local communities with one shared global federation. It is a chapter-vision page, not an active directory, branch network, application portal or map of operating locations.

## Model and boundaries

Future chapters are positioned as accountable local federation structures with locally relevant knowledge and shared global standards. They are intended to complement existing Tamil organisations, not replace them. Chapters, organisations and individual members retain distinct roles; chapter affiliation must not provide unrestricted access to private member information.

## Formation, governance and status

The page shows a proposed formation journey from community interest through review, pilot and recognition. Governance content describes proposed authority, participation, conflict, financial, audit, complaint, renewal and suspension expectations. Status labels and a future directory model remain explicitly proposed; no location is assigned a status or presented as active.

## Image and performance approach

The approved `globalChapters` registry asset appears once as the priority hero image and is captioned as a conceptual representation. The approved collaboration image appears once in the governance section as lazy media, with a clear non-operational caption. No map, geolocation or animation library is used.

## Responsive, accessibility and metadata approach

The wide hero retains a clear globe composition while stacking copy before media on smaller viewports. Journey, responsibilities, governance and readiness content move from editorial grids to linear reading sequences. The page uses one h1, semantic ordered lists, definition-list FAQ content, visible text statuses, conventional anchor targets, existing focus states and accessible active navigation. Route metadata supplies a unique canonical title, description and approved social image without structured location data.
