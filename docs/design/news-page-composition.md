# News page composition

## Purpose and current state

The News page describes the future Tamil Ulagam public newsroom: a responsible home for verified announcements, community stories, knowledge and long-term public records. It does not show a live publication feed. No approved public articles are currently presented, so the page explains the editorial foundation rather than simulating activity with placeholder records.

## Editorial model

Typed content in `apps/web/src/content/news.ts` defines the newsroom purpose, publication types, distinctions between announcements, updates, stories, resources and notices, editorial principles, publishing workflow, verification standards, authorship, safeguards, corrections, multilingual practice, accessibility, discovery, distribution, content statuses, readiness, interest routes, FAQs and final call to action.

Community stories are distinct from the homepage preview. The approved Community Stories image appears once in the News hero, captioned as a conceptual representation of future editorial storytelling. It is not presented as documentary coverage or a published story.

## Trust, publishing and public records

The composition separates institutional announcements from editorial formats and opinion, requires clear ownership and attribution, and treats facts, quotations, consent, privacy, cultural context and language review as proposed publishing responsibilities. It presents correction, update, withdrawal, archive and retraction handling as transparent public-record principles rather than a currently approved policy.

## Responsive, metadata and performance strategy

The page uses a structured navy, ivory and white reading rhythm. Dense directories become two-column or vertical reading sequences on narrower screens; the publishing workflow remains a semantic ordered list without a horizontal mobile timeline. Metadata uses the approved Community Stories image with a planned-platform description and no article schema. The hero is the only priority image; no search, CMS, editor, animation dependency, or client-side discovery interface is introduced.
