# Initiatives overview composition

## Purpose

The Initiatives overview explains Tamil Ulagam’s long-term ecosystem as one connected public vision. It gives each initiative a clear future purpose and an honest availability statement without presenting planned capabilities as operational services.

## Sequence and grouping

The page moves from an ecosystem hero to a short explanation of shared purpose, then groups the eight initiatives into human development; opportunity and economy; and knowledge, culture, and global presence. Healthcare and education receive larger editorial features. Business is the dominant opportunity feature, with jobs and tourism supporting it. Research is the dominant knowledge feature, with arts and culture and global events alongside it. The page closes with shared foundations, readiness principles, a complete directory, and participation pathways.

## Media and hierarchy

The hero uses the approved healthcare and research registry entries as a restrained montage. Each initiative appears in one detailed editorial treatment; the final directory is deliberately text-led to avoid repeated large media requests. The layout uses asymmetrical grids, numbered structural markers, deep navy feature bands, warm ivory reading surfaces, and restrained maroon and gold accents.

## Integrity and availability

Initiative identity, routes, status, summaries, and image keys remain in the central initiative content source. Overview relationships and future capability language are keyed only by existing initiative slugs. Every initiative is presented as planned, and the page explicitly states that shared platform foundations, Tamil ID, service directories, applications, bookings, listings, and registrations are not currently available.

## Responsive and accessible behaviour

Desktop compositions use broad editorial grids and controlled sticky context blocks. At narrow widths, every composition becomes a single logical vertical sequence with images retained at their intended proportions. The page uses one primary heading, semantic sections and lists, visible focus states, meaningful links, registry alt text, labelled status badges, an anchor offset for the ecosystem call to action, and no hover-only information.

## Performance and motion

Only the hero media sits at the top of the page; lower media remains lazy loaded through the shared image component. The directory avoids duplicate thumbnails. Motion is limited to existing short link, border, shadow, and image-hover transitions, with no carousel, parallax, counter, or persistent motion.
