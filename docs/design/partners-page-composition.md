# Partners page composition

## Purpose

The Partners page explains how Tamil Ulagam may build trusted relationships with organisations, institutions and professionals without implying that any relationship is active, approved or endorsed. It is an institutional discussion and governance page, not a partner directory, sales page or application portal.

## Content and status distinction

Typed content in `apps/web/src/content/partners.ts` defines the partnership vision, categories, collaboration models, boundaries, pathway, due diligence, governance, status model, initiative areas, readiness, interest routes and FAQ. Discussion, review, approval, active delivery and closure are deliberately separated. No organisation record, logo or affiliation is published.

## Governance and access

The composition makes scope, ownership, public communication, financial controls and minimum necessary data access visible. It states that partnership status never grants unrestricted access to member, chapter or organisation data. Due diligence and readiness are presented as proposed governance practices rather than final legal policy.

## Image, responsive and accessibility strategy

The approved Partnerships image appears once in the hero as a conceptual collaboration visual and is the only priority image. Structured lists become clear vertical sequences on mobile. Ordered pathway stages, text status labels, semantic FAQ markup, visible focus states and an anchored partnership-model section retain accessibility without relying on colour or interaction.

## Metadata, performance and motion

Route metadata uses the approved Partnerships image and a responsible-engagement description without relationship claims. The page is statically generated, uses no logo carousel or additional dependency, and keeps motion to existing reduced-motion-safe focus, link and media-hover treatments.
