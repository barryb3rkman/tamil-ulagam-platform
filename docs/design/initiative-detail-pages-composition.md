# Initiative detail pages composition

## Architecture and authoritative content

Each initiative detail route is built from one shared page foundation and a typed detail content source. The existing initiative source remains authoritative for identity, title, slug, route, status and primary image key. Detail content adds only page-specific purpose, audiences, future capability areas, readiness requirements, development path, partnership language, related initiatives and an editorial layout variant.

The route statically generates every approved initiative slug and resolves invalid slugs through the existing not-found path. Metadata is derived from the authoritative identity and the detail statement, with the approved primary initiative image used for social metadata.

## Editorial variants

Human development pages use a calm ivory and white environment with human-centred purpose and trust emphasis. Opportunity pages use navy capability surfaces and structured readiness treatment. Knowledge, culture and global-presence pages use white and navy editorial rhythm with restrained maroon and gold accents. The variants alter composition and emphasis without changing the shared navigation, typography, accessibility or content hierarchy.

## Page sequence

Every page uses semantic breadcrumbs, an initiative hero, planned status, purpose, intended future capabilities, audiences, trust and readiness requirements, staged development path, participation pathways, three related initiatives, previous and next navigation, and a closing call to action. Previous and next links follow the approved overview order and do not wrap at either end.

## Media, performance and motion

Each route uses only its approved registry image as the priority hero visual. No duplicate hero image is placed below the fold, and related initiative cards stay text-led to avoid unnecessary media downloads. Layout relies on responsive CSS grids and normal document flow. Motion remains limited to existing short hover, border and link transitions.

## Content integrity and accessibility

Every future service is described as planned, proposed, envisioned or subject to verified partnership and operational readiness. Detail pages never imply that appointments, courses, applications, jobs, bookings, tickets, awards, archives or registrations are active. Breadcrumbs use a labelled navigation landmark, current items are not linked, statuses are expressed in text, anchor destinations account for the header, and all navigation retains visible keyboard focus.

## Responsive strategy

Hero image and copy compositions stack deliberately below desktop widths. Audience, capability, readiness, related-initiative and previous/next layouts move from editorial grids to readable single-column sequences. Long initiative titles wrap naturally, media keeps its registry aspect ratio, and no section depends on hover or desktop-only interaction.
